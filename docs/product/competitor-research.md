# Competitor research

Research date: 2026-11-11. Scope: peer-to-peer (P2P) and managed occasionwear rental in India, with
international P2P fashion rental for contrast.

## How to read this document

Every claim carries one of three tags:

| Tag    | Meaning                                                                   |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **VF** | Verified fact, with a source link.                                        |
| **MC** | Marketing claim: the company's own statement, not independently verified. |
| **NF** | Not found or unverified. We looked and could not confirm it.              |

Some international help centres blocked automated fetching (HTTP 401/403). Where a figure comes only from a
search engine's excerpt of an official page, it is marked **(snippet)** and must be confirmed by hand before
anyone relies on it.

We have **not** invented any metric, user count, revenue figure, funding figure or market size. Where a
source states a figure, the source is named, and conflicting figures are shown side by side.

Corrections to the list we started with:

- **RoopLoop** could not be confirmed to exist as a live business.
- **Flyrobe** did not shut down; it was acquired in 2019.
- **Date The Ramp** and **Candid Knots** were not found. The nearest match to the latter, Candidmen, is a
  different, managed menswear rental business.

## Summary

The two direct competitors in our launch region, **Anokhi Reet** (Ahmedabad) and **WEVTRA** (Gujarat), are
classifieds-style listing apps that advertise 0% commission. Renters and owners chat, then settle payment,
deposit, delivery, cleaning, damage and refunds between themselves, off the platform. **SharedRobe** follows
the same model with owner subscriptions.

We found no Indian P2P occasionwear platform that takes payment and deposits on-platform and runs a dispute
process, except **SWRL**, which does so for designer and western wear rather than Gujarati ethnic wear.

That gap is the basis of Peti's differentiation hypothesis (see the end of this document). It is a
hypothesis, not a validated finding.

## 1. Anokhi Reet (anokhireet.in) — direct local competitor

- **Model.** P2P listing and inquiry platform.
  - "Anokhi Reet functions solely as a facilitator of connections" and does not "act as a party to rental
    agreements" (VF, [Terms](https://anokhireet.in/terms/)).
  - Office listed as Sidhi Vinayak Tower, Makarba, Ahmedabad. No legal entity name given (VF, same page).
- **Who handles what.**
  - "The platform does not process rental payments." Payment is by UPI, bank transfer or cash between the
    parties (VF, [FAQ](https://anokhireet.in/frequently-asked-questions/)).
  - Delivery is "mutually coordinated" (in person, Dunzo, Porter or courier); the renter usually pays (VF,
    FAQ).
  - Cleaning "is decided by both the renter and owner" (VF, FAQ).
- **Fees.**
  - "0% COMMISSION on all your rent" (MC, [homepage](https://anokhireet.in/)).
  - The Terms add that it "may charge a fee for listing products… may evolve over time" (VF, Terms).
  - Current revenue model: NF.
- **Booking.**
  - Renters pick dates, then "Make An Inquiry" or "Send a Message". "No payment required until you finish
    your conversation with the owner" (VF, [listing](https://anokhireet.in/products/AR-C6X01/),
    [How it works](https://anokhireet.in/how-it-works/english/)).
  - Owners submit listings for review and get approval over WhatsApp (VF, How it works).
  - Rental duration options: NF. Buffer days: NF. The calendar section of the listing page we fetched was
    empty.
- **Deposit, damage, late returns, cancellation.**
  - Owners "usually require a security deposit". One sample listing showed rent ₹10,000, deposit ₹10,000
    refundable, MRP ₹43,000 (VF, FAQ and listing).
  - Damage is judged by the owner; compensation "is handled entirely outside the platform"; the platform
    "does not mediate disputes" (VF, [Damage policy](https://anokhireet.in/damage-policy/)).
  - Late returns cost "daily rental charges plus a late fee", deducted by the owner (VF, FAQ).
  - Cancellations are settled privately; repeat offenders "may be suspended or banned" (VF,
    [Cancellation](https://anokhireet.in/cancellation/)).
- **Trust.**
  - "100% VERIFIED profiles" (MC). The process is not described and the Terms describe no KYC (VF, Terms).
    Login is by WhatsApp number (VF, How it works).
  - No reviews appeared on the listing we checked.
- **Geography and categories.**
  - Listings in Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Surat, Rajkot, Jamnagar, Mumbai and others (VF, homepage).
  - Women: lehenga, chaniya choli, Navratri, saree, bridal, maternity. Men: sherwani, kurta, suits. Kids.
    Also links to vendors such as makeup and mehendi (VF, homepage).
  - Positions itself as "India's first peer-to-peer marketplace for traditional Indian outfit rentals" (MC,
    [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/anokhi-reet/id6762444661)).
- **Traction and complaints.**
  - iOS listing showed 4.9 stars from 13 ratings, version 1.0 on "20 Apr" (year not shown) and a "Version
    2.0 relaunch" on 14 Oct (VF, App Store). Google Play did not render (NF).
  - Public complaints: NF.

## 2. WEVTRA (wevtra.com) — direct regional competitor

- **Model.** P2P connector: "Message the owner directly… No agents in between". Pickup or delivery is agreed
  in chat (VF, [homepage](https://www.wevtra.com/)).
- **Fees.** "0% COMMISSION"; listers "keep every rupee of the rent" (MC). Renter fees: NF.
- **Booking.** Find, chat about fit, dates and alterations, wear, return. The owner marks the rental complete
  and the renter can then rate (VF, homepage). Instant book: none seen. Durations and buffer days: NF.
- **Deposit, damage, cancellation.** The footer links to "Deposit & Refunds", "Damage Policy",
  "Cancellation" and "Hygiene Standards", but their contents did not load (NF). Payment handling: NF.
- **Trust.** "ID-checked profiles", "100% verified profiles" (MC). Ratings after return (VF).
- **Geography and categories.** Surat, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Rajkot. Bridal lehenga, saree, Navratri,
  sherwani. Web app; native apps "coming soon" (VF, homepage). Legal entity: NF.
- **Complaints.** NF.

## 4. SharedRobe (sharedrobe.in) — India-wide P2P wedding classifieds

Not to be confused with sharedrobe.app, sharedrobe.me or the US resale app of a similar name.

- **Model.** "India's peer-to-peer wedding outfit rental community… no middleman". Browse, chat, try-on
  meetup, "agree on dates and price, meet the owner" (VF, [homepage](https://www.sharedrobe.in/)). It "is
  not a party to the rental contract" (VF, [Terms](https://www.sharedrobe.in/terms)).
- **Fees.** Owner subscriptions, paid by UPI and manually verified within 24 hours (VF,
  [Pricing](https://www.sharedrobe.in/pricing)). No commission stated.

  | Plan      | Price      | Listings  |
  | --------- | ---------- | --------- |
  | Free      | ₹0         | 2         |
  | Basic     | ₹99/month  | 5         |
  | Pro       | ₹299/month | 15        |
  | Unlimited | ₹499/month | Unlimited |

- **Deposit, damage, cancellation.** "Hosts may collect a security deposit (as displayed on the listing)".
  Renters are "responsible for the outfit from pickup to return". SharedRobe "may mediate at its
  discretion"; its liability is capped at fees paid in the last 30 days. Cancellation is agreed between the
  parties (VF, Terms).
- **Trust.** "SharedRobe does not independently verify every listing". No KYC (VF, Terms).
- **Geography.** Listings seen in Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Bhopal, Mumbai and Andhra Pradesh, some priced at
  "₹2/day", which looks like test data (VF, homepage). Gujarat listings: none seen.

## 5. SWRL (swrl.co.in) — closest Indian analogue to international P2P, designer/western focus

- **Model.** P2P rent, lend, buy and sell; "Airbnb but for contemporary and designer fashion" (MC,
  [home](https://swrl.co.in/)). Private limited company registered in Faridabad, Haryana (VF,
  [T&C](https://swrl.co.in/terms-and-conditions-swrl/)).
- **Logistics.** SWRL arranges delivery and pickup (VF, [How to lend](https://swrl.co.in/how-to-lend-swrl/)).
  The lender sets an optional dry-cleaning fee (VF, [FAQ](https://swrl.co.in/faqs-swrl/)).
- **Fees — SWRL's own pages contradict each other.**
  - FAQ: "21% commission… 10% from the lender… 11% from the renter" (VF, FAQ).
  - T&C §7.1: "15% commission plus GST on all rentals" (VF, T&C). The T&C also contains the placeholder
    "[Insert Contact Email]".
- **Booking.** Lenders approve or decline requests (VF). "Minimum of 3 days" (VF, FAQ). Buffer days: NF.
- **Deposit, damage, late fees, cancellation.**
  - Deposit is "40% of the retail price" (FAQ); refund within 5 business days (T&C).
  - Damage must be reported within 24 hours. Late fee is 125% of the daily price, capped at the retail
    price; after 21 days the renter can be charged full replacement value.
  - Cancellation allowed up to 72 hours before the rental, no refund after (VF, FAQ and T&C).
  - Lenders are told to "ensure insurance cover is equivalent to the RRP" (VF, FAQ). No platform insurance.
- **Trust.** "Authenticity guaranteed" and "Every SWRLIE has been verified" (MC). The T&C says "Additional
  verification may be required" (VF).
- **Categories.** Designer bags, dresses, lehenga sets and sarees (VF). Cities: NF.

## 6. Flyrobe / Rent It Bae — history

- **History.**
  - Founded September 2015 by IIT Bombay alumni; raised $10M over two rounds, later ₹26 crore at a ₹150
    crore valuation (VF as reported by [SheThePeople, Nov 2019](https://www.shethepeople.tv/she-means-business/rent-it-bae-acquires-flyrobe/)).
  - Acquired by AARK World (Rent It Bae) in November 2019 for part cash, part stock; the combined entity was
    described as valued at "60 crores" (company statement in the same source; also
    [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.in/news/retail/rent-it-bae-acquires-flyrobe-a-clothing-rental-business/2019120123431)).
    It now trades under the Flyrobe brand.
- **Since then** (all MC, from the CEO in [Startup Pedia, Apr 2026](https://startuppedia.in/trending/everyone-thought-there-was-no-scope-in-rental-fashion-startups-now-our-yoy-growth-rate-has-jumped-from-10-to-40-says-flyrobe-ceo-11447271)):
  most stores closed during the pandemic; now "20 stores, goal 100" via franchises; inventory "80% owned /
  20% C2C". The interview's acquisition price and GMV figures conflict with the 2019 figure; treat all of
  them as unverified.
- **Model.** Hybrid: mostly owned inventory plus consigned customer outfits, online and in stores.
- **Complaints** (unverified individual posts on [Voxya](https://voxya.com/company/flyrobe-complaints/8133)):
  deposit refunds not received; lenders reporting consigned outfits and earnings not returned after the
  merger ([example](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/flyrobe-is-not-returning-my-clothes-and-money-earned/86474)).
- **Lesson for Peti.** When a platform holds owners' garments or money and the business changes, owner trust
  can be destroyed. Peti never takes custody of garments, and every rupee held is visible per booking.

## 8. International P2P (for contrast)

- **By Rotation (UK/US).** 15% from lender and 15% from renter (snippet of official
  [FAQ](https://faq.byrotation.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9303708472093-Renter-Lender-and-Buyer-Fees)). Lender
  approves or declines; renter charged only after acceptance (VF,
  [How it works](https://byrotation.com/us/how-it-works)). Minimum 3 days (VF,
  [US FAQ](https://byrotation.com/us/qa-page)). "Does not request or support deposits outside the platform"
  (VF). Lender cleans (VF). Late fee daily price + 25%, capped at replacement value (snippet). Contact the
  other party within 24 hours, escalate after 7 days (snippet). "Green Tick" for lenders with 3+ reviews
  and 4+ stars (VF). Trustpilot reviews mention damage, non-returns and refused claims over missed deadlines
  (unverified individual reviews).
- **HURR (UK).** 15% from each side on total rental cost including delivery and cleaning fees (VF,
  [help](https://help.hurrcollective.com/en/article/f3317e)). Optional damage protection. Lenders have 48
  hours to accept; photo ID check above a price threshold (snippets).
- **Tulerie (US, luxury).** 24% lending fee (snippet of [FAQ](https://www.tulerie.com/faqs)); older press
  says 17.5–17.9%. Durations 4, 10 or 20 days (third-party). Lender cleans (snippet). Members can now join
  without an interview but borrow only up to $700 retail (VF,
  [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/peer-to-peer-fashion-rental-apps-are-angling-to-go-mainstream-in-2025/)).
- **Pickle (US).** About 20% cut (VF press: Modern Retail,
  [WBUR](https://www.wbur.org/news/2026/02/13/pickle-app-clothing-rental)). Renter offers (as low as 80%
  of list), owner accepts, declines or counters; card hold until acceptance (VF,
  [help](https://help.shoponpickle.com/en/articles/7905691-how-to-rent-on-pickle)). 1 day to 4 weeks. Item
  treated as lost after 7 days unreturned.
- **Rent the Runway (US, owned inventory).** 4- or 8-day rentals, late fee up to $50/day (VF,
  [RTR](https://pixel.renttherunway.com/content/one-time-rentals)). Runs its own fulfilment and cleaning;
  fulfilment costs were 23.6% of revenue in Q4 FY25 (VF,
  [results release](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/1293642/rent-the-runway-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results)).
  Shows what owning inventory costs at scale.
- **The Volte (Australia).** Lender confirms or rejects within a window; lender cleans; "does not take
  responsibility for loss or damage"; full replacement cost after 14 days unreturned; per-listing
  cancellation policy (VF, [policy](https://thevolte.com/lending-and-lender-policy)). Commission: NF.
- **Nuuly (US).** Owned-inventory subscription. Its P2P resale app was discontinued (secondary sources).

## Patterns that look standard in P2P fashion rental

1. **Request-to-book with owner approval, not instant book** (By Rotation, HURR, Pickle, The Volte, SWRL).
   The Indian classifieds are chat-first.
2. **Payment taken or held at request and charged on acceptance** (By Rotation, HURR, Pickle). The Gujarat
   players take no payments.
3. **Total take rate around 15–30%, often split across both sides.**
4. **Minimum rental of 3–4 days**, with tiers such as 4/8 or 4/10/20. Pickle (1 day) is the outlier.
5. **Lender responsible for cleaning**, priced into the rent or a cleaning fee.
6. **Upfront deposits are the Indian norm** (SWRL 40% of retail; Anokhi Reet owner-set); international
   platforms lean on claims, card holds and optional protection instead.
7. **Short damage-reporting windows** (12–24 hours) and a **non-return threshold** that triggers replacement
   charges (7, 14 or 21 days).
8. **Late fee of about the daily price + 25%, capped at replacement value** (By Rotation, SWRL).
9. **Buffer days for cleaning: no explicit platform rule found in any verified source** (NF). Treated as an
   open design decision; Peti makes it an owner setting with a default of one day.

## Differentiation hypotheses (not validated)

- **H1 — Hold payment and deposit on-platform.** The local classifieds leave money, deposits and disputes
  to users. Holding the deposit and rent until a check-in / check-out condition report is complete may be
  the largest trust gap in Gujarat, and may justify a commission against "0% commission" rivals.
- **H2 — Standard date rules.** Request-to-book, fixed duration options, automatic cleaning buffer days.
  Removes the chat back-and-forth the classifieds rely on.
- **H3 — Timestamped condition photos at handover and return plus a fixed damage process**, informed by the
  reporting windows used internationally and by the Flyrobe complaints.
- **H4 — Lead with premium menswear (sherwani, Jodhpuri) alongside lehengas.** Anokhi Reet's copy leans
  towards women's wear and Candidmen is not in Ahmedabad. Demand is unvalidated.
- **H5 — Local, low-logistics network.** In-person handover inside Ahmedabad rather than platform
  logistics, to keep a lean cost base.
- **H6 — Plan around peak seasons** (Navratri, November–February weddings): earlier booking windows and
  tighter cancellation rules near the date.

The MVP implements H1, H2, H3 and H5 directly; H4 shapes seed data and category order; H6 is noted in
[assumptions-and-risks.md](./assumptions-and-risks.md) for later.
