# Assumptions and risks

What Peti knows, what it has decided, what it believes without proof, and what could go wrong. Review this
document before every launch decision and whenever real booking data contradicts a line in it.

> **Not legal, tax or financial advice.** Several items below concern payments regulation, tax and data
> protection in India. They are written to identify questions for qualified advisers, not to answer them.
> Nothing here should be relied on as a statement of what the law requires.

## How to read this document

| Kind                 | Meaning                                                                                     | Where it lives                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Verified fact**    | Confirmed from a cited source, or a fact about the MVP itself.                              | [Verified facts](#verified-facts)                                |
| **Product decision** | A rule we chose for the MVP (policy defaults). Deliberate, but not validated with users.    | [PRD policy defaults](./product-requirements.md#policy-defaults) |
| **Assumption**       | Something the plan depends on that we believe but have not tested.                          | [Assumptions register](#assumptions-register)                    |
| **Hypothesis**       | A bet about why Peti could win, stated so it can be proved wrong.                           | [Hypotheses](#hypotheses)                                        |

No user research, interviews or surveys have been carried out. No market sizes, user numbers or revenue
figures are used anywhere in Peti's product documents.

## Risk register

Likelihood and impact are **qualitative judgements** (Low / Medium / High) made without data. They should be
re-scored once the MVP has real bookings.

| ID   | Risk                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation (MVP and next steps)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| R-01 | **Gateway fees retained on refunded deposits.** Peti captures rental fee + deposit and refunds the deposit later; gateways commonly keep their fee on refunded amounts, so Peti pays a fee on money it never earns. On early renter cancellations and owner cancellations Peti earns nothing and may still pay the fee.                                                                                                         | High       | Medium | Get the provider's written refund-fee terms before launch. Model the cost per booking ([business model §6](./business-model.md#6-worked-examples-illustrative)). Future: deposit holds or mandates instead of capture; a renter fee; a minimum rental fee.                                                   |
| R-02 | **Regulatory constraints on collecting money for third parties.** Collecting renters' money and paying it on to owners, and holding deposits for the length of a rental, may amount to payment aggregation, which the Reserve Bank of India regulates. Settling to owners from Peti's own account may not be permitted.                                                                                                         | High       | High   | No real money movement to owners in the MVP (VF-12). Before launch: legal advice on the payments framework; use the provider's marketplace / split-settlement product (for Razorpay, its marketplace product "Route") so the provider settles to owners; confirm how deposits may be held. Not legal advice. |
| R-03 | **Tax obligations.** GST on Peti's commission; whether individual owners' rental income creates GST registration duties; e-commerce operator obligations such as TCS under section 52 of the CGST Act and TDS under section 194-O of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (or its successor provision in the new Income-tax Act). TDS compliance may require collecting owners' PAN, which conflicts with the MVP's no-ID-documents stance. | High       | High   | Tax advice before taking any real commission. Commission figures in the docs are shown before any GST. Decide how PAN or other tax details would be collected, stored and protected if required. Not tax advice.                                                                                             |
| R-04 | **SMS sender registration (DLT) in India.** Commercial SMS, including OTPs, generally requires registering the business, its sender ID and message templates on a telecom operator's DLT platform under TRAI's regulations; unregistered messages may be blocked. Without it, production phone verification does not work.                                                                                                      | High       | Medium | `SmsSender` port with a dev outbox in local/test. Before launch: register on DLT (entity, header, OTP template) through the chosen SMS provider; allow time for approval. Fallback: email-only verification would weaken FR-AUTH-07.                                                                         |
| R-05 | **Owner trust after the Flyrobe lesson.** Owners may distrust any platform that holds their money, remembering complaints about unreturned garments and earnings (VF-06).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Medium     | High   | Peti never takes custody of garments. Every rupee held is visible per booking (fee, commission, deposit, payout, status). Payout status and reference shown to the owner. Open-source code. State plainly on `/trust-and-safety` what Peti holds and when.                                                   |
| R-06 | **Hygiene and dry-cleaning expectations.** Renters expect a freshly cleaned garment; owners may not clean, or disagree about who pays after heavy use (for example after garba nights).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Medium     | Medium | Owner responsibility for cleaning stated in listing terms and how-it-works; turnaround buffer per outfit (default 1 day); condition reports note stains. Future: a cleaning-fee field and hygiene standards.                                                                                                 |
| R-07 | **Fit risk.** No try-on and no chat before confirmation, so renters may book garments that do not fit; most fit problems will surface at handover.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | High       | Medium | Typed measurements on every listing and as filters; size label; clear cancellation tiers; "not as described" dispute when measurements are wrong. Fit that matches the listing is the renter's risk, stated before payment. Future: request note, try-on appointments.                                       |
| R-08 | **No-shows at handover or return.** Owner does not turn up (renter loses the occasion) or renter does not turn up (owner loses the dates).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Medium     | High   | Contact details revealed on CONFIRMED so parties agree times; handover from the day before the start; "handover failed" disputes; owner cancellations counted; admin guideline for renter no-shows (PRD OQ-4). Reminders (FR-NOTIF-02).                                                                      |
| R-09 | **Damage disputes.** Disagreements about pre-existing marks, normal wear versus damage, and repair cost. Admin decisions may be seen as unfair.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | High       | Medium | Timestamped, immutable handover and return reports (at least 3 photos at handover); 48-hour window; claim cap at deposit minus late fee; admin sees reports side by side; written reasons; audit log.                                                                                                        |
| R-10 | **Loss or damage beyond the deposit.** A garment worth far more than its deposit is not returned or is ruined; Peti can recover at most the deposit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Medium     | High   | Deposit up to declared retail value is allowed; owner chooses; trust page and terms state the cap plainly; automatic "not returned" dispute after 3 days overdue. Future: protection add-on with an insurance partner.                                                                                       |
| R-11 | **Seasonality.** Demand may concentrate around Navratri and November–February weddings: calendars sell out, owner response windows are stressed, and quiet months leave supply idle and owners disengaged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | High       | Medium | Bookings up to 180 days ahead; date filter; turnaround buffers. Measure everything by month. Future (H6): earlier booking windows and seasonal cancellation rules.                                                                                                                                           |
| R-12 | **Competing 0%-commission classifieds and disintermediation.** Anokhi Reet and WEVTRA advertise 0% commission (MC); after a first rental, owner and renter have each other's phone numbers and can repeat off-platform.                                                                                                                                                                                                         | High       | High   | Make the on-platform value concrete (deposit held, condition reports, dispute process, reviews). Contact details only from CONFIRMED and moderation of contact details in listings. Show net earnings honestly. Avoid penalties that push users away; revisit the model if repeat bookings vanish.           |
| R-13 | **Cold-start supply in one city.** Without enough listings across categories and sizes, renters find nothing; without renters, owners leave.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | High       | High   | Owner-first launch; seed data shapes categories (H4); focus on a few categories; lean on idle bridal and groom wardrobes (P1, P2); time launch before a peak season. Do not show fake listings or invented activity.                                                                                         |
| R-14 | **Fraud.** Fake or stolen-garment listings, counterfeit designer pieces, stolen payment methods, chargebacks after a completed rental, accounts created to collect deposits.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Medium     | High   | Admin moderation of every listing; email and phone verification; payment confirmed only by verified provider capture; payouts manual and after settlement; suspension; audit log. Peti does not claim authenticity. Monitor chargebacks with the provider.                                                   |
| R-15 | **Personal safety at in-person handover.** Meeting a stranger at a home address.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Low        | High   | Address revealed only to a confirmed participant; guidance on `/trust-and-safety` (parties may agree a public meeting place by phone); suspension and reporting.                                                                                                                                             |
| R-16 | **Personal data protection.** Phone numbers, addresses and photos of homes or people; obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Medium     | High   | Data minimisation (no ID documents); EXIF/GPS stripped; contact details shown only from CONFIRMED; no secrets or personal data in logs; privacy notice. Compliance review before launch. Not legal advice.                                                                                                   |
| R-17 | **Refund failures and delays.** Provider refund fails or is slow; renters see their deposit as "lost".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Medium     | Medium | Refund outbox with retries; status shown on the booking page; failed refunds on the admin overview with manual retry; reconciliation view must show zero imbalance.                                                                                                                                          |
| R-18 | **Admin capacity, consistency and key-person risk.** One or two admins resolve disputes and mark payouts; decisions may be inconsistent or delayed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Medium     | Medium | Written dispute guidelines; every decision with notes and audit log; overview queues with ages; documented runbooks.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| R-19 | **Consumer-protection and platform rules.** Rules for e-commerce marketplaces in India (for example the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and intermediary rules under the IT Act) may require grievance officers, disclosures and complaint handling; terms may be unenforceable as drafted.                                                                                                                        | Medium     | Medium | Terms and privacy are drafts marked for legal review and versioned; accepted version stored per booking. Legal review before launch. Not legal advice.                                                                                                                                                       |
| R-20 | **Overclaiming trust.** Copy or badges imply identity or garment verification that does not exist, misleading users and creating liability.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Low        | High   | Identity status always `UNVERIFIED`; no "verified" badge other than email and phone; trust page states what is not verified; copy review before launch.                                                                                                                                                      |
| R-21 | **Policy windows that do not fit real behaviour.** Short response and payment windows, the 48-hour inspection window or the IST boundary rules frustrate users or create edge cases (for example a next-day booking approved late in the day; PRD OQ-2).                                                                                                                                                                        | Medium     | Medium | Defaults live in one policy file and are snapshotted per booking, so they can change without breaking existing bookings; boundary tests at exact IST cut-offs.                                                                                                                                               |
| R-22 | **Payment confirmation errors.** A booking confirmed without real payment, or paid but not confirmed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Low        | High   | Confirmation only by signature-verified, provider-confirmed capture; callback and webhook share one idempotent step; late captures auto-refunded; sandbox provider refuses to run in production; concurrency tests.                                                                                          |

## Before any real-money launch

These are open items, not completed work. Each blocks taking real payments or real commission:

1. Legal advice on payments regulation for a C2C marketplace collecting rent and deposits (R-02).
2. Provider onboarding, including its marketplace / split-settlement product and written terms on refund fees
   (R-01, A-16).
3. Tax advice on GST on commission, GST position of owners, TCS and TDS, and any tax details owners must
   provide (R-03).
4. DLT registration for production OTP messages (R-04).
5. Legal review of terms, privacy notice and data-protection compliance (R-16, R-19).
6. Independent security review and a tested backup and restore (PRD NFR-SEC-08, NFR-REL-05).

## Keeping this document current

- Each assumption is closed by evidence: record the date, the data used and the decision, then mark it
  validated, invalidated or replaced.
- Re-score the risk register after the first peak season and after any serious incident.
- Policy changes go into the policy file and the [PRD](./product-requirements.md#policy-defaults) together, and
  only ever apply to new bookings.
